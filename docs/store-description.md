## Short Description (30 words)

Explore a living mansion room by room. Every clue you uncover changes the dangers ahead. Choose your next room wisely — the mansion watches back.

---

## Full Description

**Land of Magic** is a single-player mansion exploration adventure built with Godot 4. You move through a gothic mansion one room at a time, investigating objects, gathering clues, and making decisions about where to go next. The twist is that the mansion is not static: every clue you find changes the state of rooms you have not yet entered. The risk and reward description of each adjacent room updates dynamically as your investigation progresses, turning every door into a meaningful strategic choice rather than a random roll.

The core loop is deliberately compact and readable in under a minute: enter a room, examine one or two objects, watch the clue panel update, and then decide whether to push deeper or secure what you have found. This rhythm makes Land of Magic ideal for sessions of twenty to thirty minutes — long enough to feel a sense of discovery, short enough to reach a natural stopping point before the demands of daily life pull you away. Players who enjoy idle combat systems and auto-battle progression will find a familiar sense of accumulation here, but the room-selection layer adds a genuine decision-making spine that passive games rarely offer.

Land of Magic is built on Godot 4.3 with a Forward Plus rendering pipeline, giving it clean visual fidelity on a wide range of hardware. The autoload architecture (SaveData and GameState) ensures your investigation state is preserved reliably between sessions, so a run interrupted by real life does not mean starting over. As the mansion evolves with each clue discovered, players will find that a second or third run through the same starting corridors unfolds in a completely different direction — making repeat playthroughs a discovery in their own right.

## Key Features

- **Clue-Driven Room Dynamics** — Investigating objects does not just reward you with information; it physically changes the risk and reward profile of rooms ahead.
- **Meaningful Door Choices** — Each adjacent room displays updated descriptions based on your current clue state. No two paths through the mansion are identical.
- **Session-Friendly Pacing** — Core loop designed for 20–30 minute sessions with clear pause points and reliable save state.
- **Roguelite Progression Layer** — XP and level-up systems reward exploration depth without punishing cautious players.
- **Godot 4.3 Native Performance** — Lightweight build with embedded PCK for zero-dependency installation on Windows.
- **Auto-Battle + Investigation Hybrid** — Combines the low-friction appeal of idle/auto-combat with the strategic texture of clue-driven exploration.
