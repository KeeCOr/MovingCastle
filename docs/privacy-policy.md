# Land of Magic — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-09-05
**Developer:** Stoicent
**Game:** Land of Magic / Living Mansion (Godot 4.3)
**Contact:** koheag2@stoicent.com

---

## Overview

Land of Magic is a singleplayer, offline game. It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with respect to user information.

---

## Data We Do Not Collect

Land of Magic does **not** collect any of the following:

- Personal identifiers (name, email address, account credentials)
- Device identifiers (device ID, advertising ID, IP address)
- Location data (precise or approximate)
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Gameplay statistics or session data sent to external systems
- Crash reports transmitted to external servers
- Any data that could be used to identify or track you

---

## No Network Activity

Land of Magic operates entirely offline. The game:

- Does not connect to the internet during gameplay
- Does not connect to any external server, analytics service, or third-party API
- Does not transmit data of any kind to the developer or any third party

> **Note on engine:** Land of Magic is built with Godot 4.3 and exported as a standalone portable executable with embedded PCK. All game logic — room state, clue tracking, player progression — runs locally with no outbound connections.

---

## Local Save Data

Land of Magic stores game state locally on your device via Godot's built-in save system (SaveData autoload). This includes:

- Room investigation progress and clue states
- Player XP and level progression
- Current run state and GameState data

This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not accessible to the developer
- Can be deleted at any time by removing the game's user data directory (`%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\LivingMansion\` on Windows)

---

## No In-App Purchases

Land of Magic does not offer in-app purchases, premium currency, loot boxes, or any monetization system that involves user financial data.

---

## No Third-Party SDKs or Advertising

Land of Magic does not include:

- Advertising networks or ad SDKs
- Analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics)
- Social media SDKs or share integrations
- Any third-party GDScript plugin or addon that accesses or transmits user data

> The GUT (Godot Unit Test) addon is included in the project for development testing only and is excluded from release exports via the export preset filter `addons/gut/*,tests/*`.

---

## Children's Privacy

Because Land of Magic collects no data of any kind, it is safe for players of all ages and complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and applicable regional children's privacy regulations.

---

## Steam Platform

If you purchase Land of Magic through Steam, Valve Corporation's privacy policy applies to your Steam account and transaction. That policy is separate from this one and governs how Valve handles your data. Stoicent receives no personal data from Steam.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If Land of Magic is ever updated to collect data (for example, optional cloud saves), this policy will be updated and a new effective date will be published before that version ships.
